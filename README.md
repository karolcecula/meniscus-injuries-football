# football-injury-analysis

## Research question
What patterns characterize injuries in professional football, how do they vary across different player groups, what are their consequences for player and team performance, and to what extent can elevated injury risk be predicted before an injury occurs?

## Who could benefit from this analysis?

This analysis may be particularly valuable for football clubs, coaching staffs, performance analysts, strength and conditioning coaches.

By examining injury patterns, match exposure, training load, player characteristics, and the consequences of injuries, the project aims to provide a better understanding of how workload and player availability relate to injury risk and team performance.

Such insights could support clubs in making more informed decisions about player workload, recovery, training intensity, squad rotation, and return-to-play management. Rather than attempting to eliminate injuries entirely, the goal is to identify patterns and periods of elevated risk that may help staff manage players more effectively and reduce avoidable injury exposure.

From a practical perspective, this type of analysis could support coaching  by helping them evaluate whether current training and match demands are sustainable and where adjustments in workload management may be beneficial.
